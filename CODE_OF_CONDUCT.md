# Code of conduct

zodcat is a small developer tool and a small community. Keep discussions technical, direct, and respectful.

## Expected behavior

- Assume good faith and critique ideas, not people.
- Give enough context for others to reproduce a problem.
- Respect different levels of experience.
- Keep issues and pull requests focused on the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or imagery.
- Publishing another person's private information without consent.
- Repeated off-topic or disruptive behavior after a request to stop.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments and contributions, close issues, or block accounts when this policy is violated. Report concerns to the maintainers through GitHub. Reports will be handled as privately as the platform allows.

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces.
